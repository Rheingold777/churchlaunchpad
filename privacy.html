Privacy Notice

How information is handled when you use this website.

Assessment Requests

The assessment form asks for a church name, your name, email address, location, and the services you want help with. The form is submitted through FormSubmit so ChurchLaunchpad can receive and respond to the request.

Analytics

The homepage currently loads Google Analytics to measure visits and site use. Google may process device, browser, and interaction information under its own privacy terms.

How Information Is Used

Information you submit is used to review your request, communicate with you, and provide the assessment or related services you asked about. ChurchLaunchpad does not ask for payment information through this form.

External Services

This website links to and uses external services. Those providers handle information under their own policies when you use their services.

Sensitive Information

Do not send passwords, financial account numbers, government identification numbers, medical records, or other highly sensitive documents through the website form or ordinary email.

Questions or Corrections

For questions about information you submitted, email hello@churchlaunchpad.org.

Last reviewed August 7, 2026. Review this notice whenever forms, analytics, hosting, or external services change.
